# Supraspinatus tendon and subacromial bursa

Source: 2025-03-01 Dr. Example《Shoulder Ultrasound Workshop》· 20250301_Example_Shoulder-Ultrasound · c02

## Positioning

Ask the person to place the hand behind the back pocket so the tendon moves out from under the
acromion [c02 01:20]. Scan in both the long and the short axis of the tendon [c02 03:00].

## Bursa

A normal bursa is a thin dark line between two bright layers. Fluid that widens it, or bunching
of the bursa against the acromion when the arm is raised, is worth recording [c02 12:40].
