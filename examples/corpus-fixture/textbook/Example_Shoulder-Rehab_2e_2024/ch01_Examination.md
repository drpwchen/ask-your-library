# Chapter 1 Examination of the Shoulder

## Inspection

Look at both shoulders from the front and from behind with the upper body uncovered. Compare the
height of the shoulders, the contour of the deltoid and the position of the scapulae at rest.
Wasting above or below the spine of the scapula suggests a long-standing problem of the
supraspinatus or infraspinatus.

<!-- page 12 -->
## Range of motion

Measure active motion first, then passive motion. When passive motion is clearly larger than
active motion, weakness or pain limits the movement; when both are reduced to the same degree,
the joint or its capsule is stiff.

| Movement | Typical range (degrees) |
|---|---|
| Flexion | 0–180 |
| Abduction | 0–180 |
| External rotation | 0–90 |

<!-- page 13 -->
## Strength

Test each rotator cuff muscle against resistance and compare with the other side. Record pain and
weakness separately: pain with full strength and weakness without pain point to different problems.
