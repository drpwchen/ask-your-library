# Chapter 2 Exercise Progression

## Early phase

Start with movements that do not provoke lasting pain, such as pendulum swings and assisted
elevation with the other arm. The aim of this phase is to keep motion while irritated tissue settles.

<!-- page 15 -->
## Strengthening phase

Add resistance once active motion is comfortable. Begin with low loads and many repetitions, and
raise the load gradually from week to week. Pain that lasts into the next day means the step was
too large.

<!-- page 16 -->
## Return to activity

Practise the movements of the person's own work or sport. Progress when the previous level is
completed without symptoms on two consecutive sessions.
