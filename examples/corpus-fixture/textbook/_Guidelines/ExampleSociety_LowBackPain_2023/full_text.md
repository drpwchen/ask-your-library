# Example Society Guideline: Low Back Pain (2023)

## Recommendation 1

For people with acute low back pain, advise staying active and continuing usual activities as far
as possible. Bed rest is not advised as a treatment.

<!-- page 2 -->
## Recommendation 2

Offer a structured exercise programme to people with chronic low back pain. The type of exercise
can follow the person's preference; regular attendance matters more than the specific programme.

## Recommendation 3

Reassess people whose pain does not improve as expected, and look again for signs of a specific
cause that would need a different approach.
